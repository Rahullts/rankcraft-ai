# An Introduction to Growing an Indoor Herb Garden

Growing fresh herbs inside your home is a rewarding hobby that enhances your kitchen. Many homeowners enjoy having plants on their windowsill.

Indoor herb gardens need some care. You should place them where there is light and water them when necessary. You can grow basil, mint, and parsley. It is good to have nice pots with drainage.

In summary, having an indoor herb garden will make your cooking taste better and bring green life into your kitchen.
